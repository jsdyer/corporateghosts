What’s in the index

The index covers the four English federal downloads from Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada. It includes legal names, published CRA business numbers, registration locality, governing legislation, and published status. Choose a subset for a smaller first download.

Inactive or dissolved status is a legal record, not a finding of misconduct. Name forms are the current export’s legal-name variants, not a complete renaming history. Use the official register to investigate previous legal names. Provincial and territorial corporations are outside this dataset.

    CURRENT FEDERAL RECORD

    Check a corporation or CRA business number

    Enter a federal corporation number or a nine-digit CRA business number (BN). A BN is matched to its published federal corporation number before requesting current details. Use the provincial links by the name search for BC registrations and Nova Scotia co-operatives.

    Nine digits are treated as a CRA business number. Spaces, hyphens and CRA program-account suffixes such as RC0001 are accepted. Select Federal corporation number to override detection.

    A legal registration does not prove a website belongs to that business, operates honestly, or is currently trading. A missing federal match may be registered provincially or under separate financial legislation.
